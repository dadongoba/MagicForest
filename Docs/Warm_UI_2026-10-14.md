## 实际源码

新增 ForestUITheme.h/.cpp，统一浅纸色、暖棕字、橄榄绿操作按钮、输入框与下拉菜单各状态。SForestPaperBorder 使用文件贴图九宫拉伸，不再在 OnPaint 里画枝叶。

ForestHarvestMagic.cpp/.h：背包为 SWrapBox，110×120 格子、8px 间距，左侧59%/右侧41%；不足12格补无交互空槽。道具仍手动点击查看名称、元素、点数、采集日和描述。火/风/水使用通用水彩标记，并非道具本体图。采集提示、HUD采用纸页容器。炼药保留原蓝图结构，仅统一文字、底色与按钮。

ForestNPCInteraction.cpp：共享主题用于找人、确认与对话。保留选择目标、手动选择支付物品、等待AI、扣费/退款与关闭流程。本次无玩法与NPC人设更改。

Build.cs 新增两张PNG的 NonUFS RuntimeDependencies，打包时作为松散文件分发，读取路径基于 ProjectContentDir；不需要启动编辑器导入 uasset。缺图时纸页回退纯色、元素标记隐藏，名称/属性仍显示。打包和实际PNG加载未做运行验证。

## 生成方法与提示词

使用内置 imagegen 技能/工具，以用户设计板为风格参考。未使用CLI/API密钥。最终采用素材分别为 WoodlandPaper.png 与 ElementEmblems.png。

纸页主提示：Create ONE production UI texture asset, not a UI mockup, using supplied image as STYLE reference. A large empty landscape parchment sheet panel for a cozy woodland RPG, aspect ratio 3:2, filling almost all canvas with transparent outside narrow margins. Match reference inventory pages and dialogue paper exactly in material spirit: pale creamy handmade watercolor paper, very subtle soft ochre fibers and translucent uneven washes, fine organically imperfect sepia contour, gently worn and curled corners, layered thin paper edges visible at bottom. Mostly rectangular with subtly irregular silhouette, corners only slightly rounded. Central 80 percent must be very light cream almost blank and uniform for text readability. Small quiet olive botanical sprig at lower RIGHT corner only within edge 8 percent, painted opaque leaves, not line clip art. No text, symbols, lettering, icons, objects, grids, gradients, glow, drop shadow halo or UI contents. Flat front view. Refined restrained professional game UI asset, maintain delicate brushwork, avoid saturated brown, avoid orange, avoid modern rounded cards. Outside actual paper is alpha transparent.

纸页后处理提示：Precisely remove ONLY all tan background outside the actual paper sheet silhouette. Outside the layered paper edges must be truly transparent alpha=0, no beige background, shadow or halo. Keep paper sheet, contour, flowers, internal creamy paper texture exactly unchanged. This is UI asset background extraction, not new illustration.

元素图集提示：Production game UI icon sprite strip. Exactly three small hand-painted watercolor ELEMENT EMBLEMS on fully transparent background, horizontal row, equally sized and evenly spaced with centers x=1/6,1/2,5/6 of canvas, each contained entirely within its own third, no overlap. Canvas 3:1 wide. Style must match supplied reference's tiny inventory watercolor objects: gentle earthy muted pigments, slightly imperfect sepia pencil contours, illustrated storybook RPG, no glossy digital shading. LEFT emblem: a small warm terracotta flame with ochre core, clearly FIRE, no logs. CENTER emblem: a curled olive-grey breeze with a delicate pale feather, clearly WIND. RIGHT emblem: one blue-grey translucent droplet with tiny ripples, clearly WATER. No circles, frames, cards, backgrounds, writing, letters, numerals, grid, UI or labels. Each drawing has generous blank transparent margin. Not distinct inventory item illustrations: these are reusable elemental emblems.
